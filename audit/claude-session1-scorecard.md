## Consequential corrections before a public write-up

1. **Keep source inference separate from measurement.** The numerical mapping is strong. Gene-symbol differences suggest some annotation differences, but not that every difference is a rename or that a distinct pipeline has been demonstrated. High same-patient expression correlation and Y-marker patterns do not prove specimen identity or biological-versus-technical replication. The available GEO descriptions and supplement do not provide the GSM-level specimen key needed to resolve that question.
2. **Revise the power argument.** Describe the exact rank-test floor and Bonferroni limitation accurately; remove the categorical BH impossibility claim. Label t-test calculations as illustrative independent-sample assumptions, not measured power for a batch-confounded RNA-seq model. With outcome fixed within batches 1 and 2 in the PRE core, batch-respecting permutation has little support.
3. **Show both analysis populations and design ranks.** Claude chose a defensible pretreatment question, but the prompt also requested treatment-state and batch overlap. Add a reference table for ON biopsies and explicitly distinguish full mathematical rank from weak within-batch biological support. No after-the-fact gene analysis is needed to correct the review.
